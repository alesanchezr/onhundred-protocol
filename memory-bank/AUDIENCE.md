# Onhundred Audience

Who Onhundred speaks to, which 4Geeks buyer personas it overlaps with, and which B2B buyers could pay for the protocol, its implementation or its tools.

Last updated: Oct 9, 2026.

## Sources

- **4Geeks CMS products** (4geeks.com and business.4geeks.com share the same three products): AI Fluency, AI Engineering and AI Flex, each with full personas (fears, internal dialogue, objections, aspiration, buying behavior). The CMS is the source of truth for persona detail; this file only summarizes and maps them.
- **4Geeks Marketing Plan 2026** (Marcelo Ricigliano, Google Drive), section 4: short B2C and B2B personas by market (USA, Spain, LATAM).
- **PRODUCT.md**: Onhundred's own three user groups (worried professionals, founders and small business owners, technical builders).

## Decisions so far

- **Life and work both stay in.** Personal and Relationships loops drive virality; Company loops drive monetization. The quick guide covers all three branches.
- **No code by default.** Guides use copy-paste prompts, no-code tools and templates. Technical options (vibe coding tools, building your own agents) can appear as clearly optional extras.
- **The protocol is for deep readers** (technical builders, journalists, coordinators). A separate quick guide serves people who want to start fast.
- **No offers inside Onhundred content.** The relationship with 4Geeks is disclosed; the path to 4Geeks products happens outside the guide and the protocol.

## B2C personas: fit with Onhundred

### Strong fit: first readers of the quick guide

| Persona (product) | Who | Why it fits | What they need from the guide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Business Owner Who Does It All (AI Fluency) | Owner of a 1 to 10 person business, 32 to 50, works 10 to 14 hours a day, $30K to $200K/year | He is the bottleneck and "tried ChatGPT but had no system." Buys on hours recovered and tasks automated, which is the protocol's time freed metric | A system, not tips. Concrete hours on day 1. Proof from similar small businesses |
| The Non-Technical Professional (AI Fluency) | Manager or coordinator in a non-technical function, 30 to 45, company already demands AI adoption | Matches PRODUCT.md's "worried professional." Fears being exposed or replaced, and not finishing | "No coding required" everywhere. Early easy wins. Cycles as accountability |

### Medium fit: deep readers of the protocol or the tools

| Persona (product) | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The Technical Who Wants to Lead (AI Fluency) | Natural protocol coordinator inside a company; wants the strategic layer, not more tools |
| The Entrepreneur (AI Engineering) | Hit the limits of no-code, wants to build the agents; PRODUCT.md's technical builder at delegate.onhundred.com |
| The Professional Adding AI to Their Career (AI Fluency) | A public log can become proof of hybrid AI skills |
| The Semi-Technical Professional (AI Engineering) | Fears IT and analysis work being automated; could apply the protocol to their own role |

### Weak fit

| Persona (product) | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The Business Owner Ready to Grow (AI Fluency) | Wants revenue growth, not hours back; only partly served by net savings |
| The Career Changer, The Frustrated Graduate, The Migrant, All or Nothing (AI Engineering) | They need a job, not a delegation method. Not a target for the guide |
| AI Flex personas (The Explorer, The Developer Who Wants to Add AI, The Recent Graduate, The Technical Professional from Another Field) | Not yet reviewed in detail |

## B2B: what can be sold

| Offer | What it is | Who it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Protocol | Free and open. Builds credibility and is the method everything else runs on | Everyone; journalists and evaluators |
| Implementation | Diagnostic (inventory and triage by area), running cycles with a protocol coordinator, AI Fluency training around each role's delegated tasks | Companies, institutions |
| Tools | Agents, prompts and workflows from delegate.onhundred.com; log and aggregate grid software | Builders, IT teams, implementation partners |

## B2B personas: hypotheses to validate

These are proposals, not validated personas. The 4Geeks marketing plan only has three short B2B profiles (manager or team lead, corporate L&D buyer, institutional or government buyer); the rest are new.

| Persona | Pain | What Onhundred offers them | Likely offer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Head of L&D or People | Mandated to show AI adoption; training does not change behavior | Adoption measured by loops delegated per role, not course completion. Section 12.1 rules (no performance use, voluntary, data ownership) make it safe to roll out | Implementation + AI Fluency + AI Flex seats |
| COO or transformation lead | Spent on AI and cannot show ROI | Triage by area as a diagnostic; time freed and net savings computed in code | Diagnostic, then implementation |
| Manager or team lead | Needs their team AI-fluent with little budget | A pilot with their own team; the protocol is free | Pilot, then implementation |
| SMB founder (10 to 50 people) | Is the bottleneck, and so is each key employee | Applies it to themselves first, then each employee; same method at both levels | Implementation, tools |
| CIO, CTO or IT lead | Shadow AI, data risk, no governance | Data handling rules, ceilings enforced in code, a log per application | Tools, integration |
| Institutional or government workforce buyer | Needs measurable outcomes from reskilling funds | A public, measurable method with per-area results | Implementation bundled with all three programs |
| University or curriculum designer | Needs current, applied AI courses | The protocol as course content ("delegate your own work"), authored in LearnPack | Licensing, LearnPack |
| AI consultancy or implementation partner | Needs a repeatable method to sell | A published, credible method with templates and agents | Partner program, tools |

**Not buyers but gatekeepers:** works councils and employee representatives (Spain and Europe), and compliance teams in banks. The consent and data rules are written to pass them.
