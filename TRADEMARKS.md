# Trademarks

The code is licensed under Apache 2.0 and the protocol text under CC BY 4.0. Neither license covers the Onhundred name, the ON100 mark or the logo. This page says how you can use them.

## You can

- Say you follow the Onhundred Protocol, or that your project is based on it.
- Run your own log from this template and keep the Onhundred name on it.
- Use the "Follows the Onhundred Protocol" badge in `public/badge.svg` on your site.
- Write about the protocol, review it, teach it or criticize it.

Each time, credit it like this, with the link:

> Based on the Onhundred Protocol by Ale Sánchez, [onhundred.com](https://onhundred.com)

Keep the credit in the footer of your log page. It is on by default.

## You can't

- Name your own product, fork, company or service "Onhundred", or anything close enough to be confused with it.
- Use the logo or the ON100 mark as your own brand.
- Suggest that Ale Sánchez or Onhundred endorses, certifies or runs your version when they don't.
- Remove the credit from a public log page while still calling it the Onhundred Protocol.

If you change the protocol's rules, you can still say your version is based on the Onhundred Protocol. Say what you changed.
